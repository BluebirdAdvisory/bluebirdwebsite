## Resource efficiency

We strive to use resources efficiently and minimise waste. This includes reducing energy consumption and water usage. Bluebird operates without using paper.

## Coworking and digital commuting

When we are not required to work on site with our clients, we use a coworking space to reduce our environmental footprint. This allows us to share resources and reduce our energy consumption. We promote remote working arrangements and digital communication tools.

## Sustainable procurement

We consider environmental sustainability when selecting our suppliers and products. We prioritise products and services that have minimal environmental impact and use them in a way that prolongs their lifespan.
