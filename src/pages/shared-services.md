## The reality for many teams

Many organisations need EA, admin or HR support, but not at a full-time, permanent level. What we see most often:

- Workload is real but uneven
- EA, admin and HR tasks are fragmented across the team
- Hiring full-time creates fixed costs without full utilisation
- Important admin work competes with higher-value roles

## The cost problem with full-time hiring

A permanent hire means paying for more than just productive hours. Typically, you are also paying for:

- Superannuation
- Recruitment and onboarding costs
- Downtime during quiet periods
- Coverage gaps when that person is unavailable

For many teams, this results in ongoing cost without consistent workload.

## A contracting-style alternative

Bluebird’s on-demand EA and business admin support operates like contracting, not employment. This means:

- No superannuation
- No paid leave or public holidays
- No long-term employment commitment
- No cost when the work is not there

You pay **only for the time used**, at an agreed hourly or daily rate.

## Why the shared service model works

Instead of hiring one full-time EA, admin or HR resource, you access experienced support as a shared service. You get:

- The right level of support, not excess capacity
- Coverage across EA, admin and HR tasks
- Flexibility to scale hours up or down
- Clear boundaries on time and cost

## What this looks like in practice

### Scenario 1: Not enough work for a full-time EA

You do not need a full-time EA, but your inbox needs regular attention, your calendar needs coordination, and follow-ups and admin are slowing you down.

**Shared service outcome:** short, regular check-ins for inbox and diary; one or two deeper admin blocks per week; consistent support without a full-time salary.

### Scenario 2: Admin and HR work spread across the team

Admin and HR tasks are handled by consultants, managers, or whoever has capacity that week. This is costly and inefficient.

**Shared service outcome:** centralised admin and HR support; delivery staff focus on their core roles; managers stop chasing timesheets and forms.

### Scenario 3: 1 day per week (8 hours), spread across the week

A common setup for small teams. Typical coverage: daily inbox triage and urgent actions; meeting coordination and follow-ups; timesheet reminders and checks; light HR admin (onboarding or offboarding steps).

**Result:** admin stays under control without the overhead of a permanent role.
