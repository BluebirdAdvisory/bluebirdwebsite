## Respect for human rights

We are committed to respecting the human rights of our employees, customers, and suppliers. We do not engage in any form of discrimination or forced labour.

## Diversity and inclusion

We value and champion diversity and inclusion in our workplace and are already a diverse team despite our small size. We work to develop a corporate culture in which everyone is accepted and treated with respect, regardless of their race, gender, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic.

## Ethical business practices

We conduct our business in an ethical and transparent manner. We adhere to all applicable laws and regulations and do not engage in any practices that are illegal or unethical.
