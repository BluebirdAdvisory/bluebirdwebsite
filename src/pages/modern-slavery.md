Bluebird recognises the pervasive issue of modern slavery on a global scale and acknowledges the imperative for widespread transformation. As an organisation centred on people and guided by values, Bluebird's initial Modern Slavery Statement articulates our dedication to heightening our understanding of modern slavery, enhancing our methods for risk assessment and mitigation, and fortifying our adherence to compliance and accountability standards.
