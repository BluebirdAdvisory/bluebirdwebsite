The data

Source
Synthetic responses modelled on the National AI Centre’s AI adoption tracker. They are not real survey results.
Sample
400 responses a month from May 2024 to September 2026, 11,600 in total. Owners and decision makers in businesses with 0 to 500 employees.
Adopting AI
Using AI broadly, using it in part of the business, or implementing it now. A working definition for this demonstrator.
Weighting
Percentages are weighted by business size to match the national business mix. n is the unweighted number of responses.
Small groups
Figures from fewer than 10 responses are never shown. Below 30, the dashboard flags the figure and hides its change from the previous quarter.
Change over time
Each month surveys different businesses, so small movements between quarters may not be meaningful.
